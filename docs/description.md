# Lunar Lander

Fly your lander down to a landing pad before the fuel runs out. Touch down slowly, upright and on a pad, or you crash. Inspired by the 1979 Atari arcade game.

## Features
- 30 levels of generated terrain, plus a two-level tutorial
- 9,999 seeds to explore, each its own set of 30 levels (seed 1 is the original set)
- Pads far from the drop point are narrower but pay more: 1x, 2x, 3x or 5x
- Your score comes from the fuel you have left and the pad's multiplier
- Three button thrust modes: Tap Impulse (tap for a burst), Binary (hold) and Ramp (hold and the thrust builds), on Up or OK
- Four tilt modes for the Video Game Module: tilt the Flipper to steer, and in Full Tilt mode tilt it forward to fire the engine
- Fuel modes: refuel every level, or one tank for the whole game (500, 350 or 200)
- Difficulty presets (Easy, Medium, Hard, Realistic), or set your own landing limits
- Sound and vibration, each Off, Low, Med or High
- High score and settings are saved to the SD card
- TV mode corrects the stretched picture on the Video Game Module's HDMI output
- HDMI audio out: on a Video Game Module running the VGM480 firmware, the sounds play on the TV

## Controls
- Left and Right: rotate the lander
- Up (or OK, set in Settings): fire the engine, depending on the thrust mode
- OK on the result banner: next level, or retry after a crash
- Back while flying: tap to pause (Resume, Zero tilt in the tilt modes, Quit to menu), or hold 1 second to return to the menu
- On the menu's title bar, the lander icon opens the high score, the title opens About and the wrench opens Settings

## Requirements
Plays on any Flipper Zero. The tilt modes need the Flipper Zero Video Game Module.
