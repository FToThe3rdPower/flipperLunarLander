## v1.0
First release on the Flipper Apps Catalog.

- 30 levels (up from 10), ending on a victory screen
- High score saved to the SD card; open it from the lander icon on the menu
- Two-level tutorial that adapts to the chosen thrust mode and difficulty
- Difficulty presets (Easy, Medium, Hard, Realistic) and custom landing limits
- 3x and 5x pads are narrower, and only a pad's own width is flat
- The crash banner shows the touchdown speeds and why you crashed
- Sound and vibration levels: Off, Low, Med, High
- Settings and menu choices are saved to the SD card
- Back while flying: hold 1 second to return to the menu; in tilt modes a tap re-zeroes the tilt
- TV mode corrects the stretched picture on the Video Game Module's HDMI output
- Fixed controls sticking when the lander touched the left or top edge of the screen
- Smoother play with the Video Game Module connected to a TV
- Optional debug HUD
